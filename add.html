A new album or playlist can be made without a track. A track in no album or playlist only shows under “All tracks”.

3. Your new library.json

  1. Click Copy (or Download) below.
  2. On GitHub, open library.json, click the pencil icon, select everything (Ctrl or Cmd+A), paste, and click Commit changes. If you used Download, you can instead choose Add file, then Upload files, and drop the downloaded file in to replace the old one.
  3. Wait about a minute and refresh the player. If you added an album, upload its cover image to your repo next to index.html too.
